## Prompt
> A painterly portrait of a sixteen-year-old girl lamplighter at dusk on a wet stone sea wall. She is short and wiry, with copper-red hair cropped very close to her head, a narrow freckled face, pointed chin, straight dark brows and grey-green eyes. She wears a heavy grey wool coat that is far too big for her, hanging past her knees, with a row of brass buttons each stamped with a small lantern. The back of her left hand shows a shiny, healed burn scar. She stands beside a tall glass-cylinder street lantern on an iron post, with a small white enamel plate reading "40"; the lantern burns with a cold blue-white flame, and its light makes the wet stones shine like fish scales. Behind the wall, a thick pale fog like wet wool presses close, its edge curling toward the light. She holds a small brass trimming knife and looks out into the fog, wary but steady. Three-quarter view, waist up, muted grey and slate tones, the lantern light the brightest thing in the image.
