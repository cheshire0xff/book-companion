# Nell Harrow

## Established details
- Sixteen years old, ten years after the night of the Prologue; she lights Ellamere's salt lanterns herself — [The Tidewatch, part 1 chapter 1]
- An apprentice lamplighter: the scar on her hand dates from "her first winter as an apprentice" — [The Tidewatch, part 1 chapter 1]
- Short and wiry, with copper hair cut close to her skull so it will not catch fire — [The Tidewatch, part 1 chapter 1]
- A shiny burn scar across the back of her left hand — [The Tidewatch, part 1 chapter 1]
- Wears her father's grey wool coat, which comes down past her knees; its brass buttons are stamped with the guild's lantern — [The Tidewatch, part 1 chapter 1]; still wears it, pulled over her knees, in [The Drowned Bell, chapter 2]
- Walks the round from lantern one, by the guild hall, to lantern ninety-nine at the harbor mouth, trimming wicks and topping up brine; Master Corwin Ashby follows her — [The Tidewatch, part 1 chapter 1]
- Spends her mornings in the guild cellar dipping wicks in brine — [The Tidewatch, part 1 chapter 2]
- Relit lantern sixty-one, whose glass was cracked from the inside — [The Tidewatch, part 1 chapter 3]
- Worked the Mother Lamp's brine valve through the storm night, when Corwin's hands shook too badly — [The Tidewatch, part 2 chapter 3]
- Watched Fenn's dockhands take the guild's salt sacks at night, and told Corwin — [The Drowned Bell, chapter 2]
- Knows the lamplighter's knot; she learned it "at six years old, standing on the guild steps" — [The Drowned Bell, chapter 3]

## Reasonable inferences
- She is the six-year-old daughter who held Tobin Harrow's rope in the Prologue: the shared surname, "ten years later", Corwin speaking to her of "your father" in the Tidewatch, and learning the knot at six on the guild steps — based on [The Tidewatch, Prologue; part 1 chapter 1; part 2 chapter 1] and [The Drowned Bell, chapter 3]
- The coat was her father's, kept since he was lost in the Murk — based on [The Tidewatch, part 1 chapter 1]

## Unknown / not established
- Eye colour, face, skin tone and exact height.
- Whether the burn came from a salt lantern specifically.

## Continuity notes
- As of The Drowned Bell, chapter 3, she is still an apprentice and still wears the grey coat.
