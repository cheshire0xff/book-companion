# Tamsin Reed

## Established details
- A cartographer's apprentice; she carries a map case that bangs against her hip — [The Tidewatch, part 1 chapter 2]
- A head taller than Nell, with dark brown skin and black hair in dozens of small braids tied off with blue thread — [The Tidewatch, part 1 chapter 2]
- Ink-stained fingers; round spectacles mended at the bridge with twine — [The Tidewatch, part 1 chapter 2]
- Has mapped the Murk for a year, from the sea wall at dusk, with her fogglass: a brass tube with a lens of smoky crystal that shows the fog's slow currents as faint lines, like grain in wood — [The Tidewatch, part 1 chapter 2]
- Draws the Murk's edge for every night in pencil, one line over another; she marked the nights the fog drew back far from the town with the word "bell" — [The Tidewatch, part 2 chapter 2]
- During the storm she called the gaps in the lanterns down from the top of the Tidewatch through a speaking-trumpet, then hauled brine buckets up its steps for the Mother Lamp — [The Tidewatch, part 2 chapter 3]
- Worked out the day of the low tide for the drowned quay, and spotted the straight rope line in the fog through her fogglass — [The Drowned Bell, chapter 3]
