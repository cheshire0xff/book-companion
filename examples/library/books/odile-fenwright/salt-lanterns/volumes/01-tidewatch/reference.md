# The Tidewatch: reference

Odile Fenwright, The Salt Lanterns, volume 1. Spoilers for this whole book. Source: this library's `source.epub`, read with `book-search`.

## Structure and chronology

- Prologue (ten years before), then Part One (3 chapters) and Part Two (3 chapters, with an interlude nested in chapter 2). Chapter numbering restarts in each part.
- Chronological order: Tobin lost in the Murk (Prologue) → nine years before the main story, Corwin hears the bell and hides it (Interlude) → Tamsin starts mapping the Murk (a year before chapter 2) → main story over some weeks, ending on the last night of autumn and the dawn after.

## World and rules

- **Ellamere:** a harbor town on the Grey Reach coast, with a guild hall and a sea wall; an "old drowned quay" lies under the harbor water.
- **The Murk:** a nightly fog from the Grey Reach, thick and pale like wet wool. It creeps, turns corners and finds open doors. Characters believe it listens (Tobin's belief, reported by Corwin).
- **Salt lanterns:** tall glass cylinders on iron posts with numbered enamel plates, burning brine-soaked wicks with blue-white light. Salt fire repels the Murk; ordinary fire does not. There are 99 on the wall, lit with salt matches, plus the Mother Lamp.
- **The Tidewatch:** the oldest lighthouse on the Grey Reach, a black basalt tower at the end of the sea wall with 212 steps. It holds the **Mother Lamp**, a man-tall salt lantern fed from a copper tank by a brine valve. It keeps a lamp-room log.
- **Fogglass:** Tamsin's brass tube with a smoky-crystal lens that shows the Murk's currents as faint lines.
- **The bell:** heard out past the harbor mouth, and at the end from beneath the drowned quay. When it rings, the Murk draws back from the town. Source unknown.

## Threads and open questions

- Who or what rings the bell, and why does the Murk retreat when it rings?
- What cracked lantern sixty-one from the inside? Is someone or something putting lanterns out (as on the Prologue night)?
- Why has Corwin hidden the bell for nine years, and why keep the cracked lantern from the harbormaster?
- What happened to Tobin Harrow? His rope was cut "clean", which suggests a deliberate cut (inference).
- The Murk is pushing closer to the wall.

## End state

- The town survived the storm on the Mother Lamp alone. Nell and Tamsin know the bell is real and that it sounds from under the drowned quay. Corwin's log shows he has known for years; the book doesn't say whether Nell has seen it.
