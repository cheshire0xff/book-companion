## Prompt
> A wide establishing shot of an old lighthouse at dusk at the end of a long curved stone sea wall. The lighthouse is a squat, heavy tower of black basalt, topped by a lamp room of glass panes crusted white with salt. From the lamp room, a cold blue-white light spreads out over the harbor mouth in a wide pale fan. Beyond the wall, a thick pale fog like wet wool rolls in from a grey sea and presses toward the wall; where the fan of light touches it, the fog thins like breath on a cold window. Along the wall, a line of tall glass-cylinder lanterns on iron posts burns the same blue-white. Two tiny figures walk the wall toward the tower. Slate roofs of a cliffside harbor town at the left edge. Moody, painterly, cool palette of slate, black and fog-white.
