# The Tidewatch

## Established details
- The oldest lighthouse on the Grey Reach, at the end of Ellamere's sea wall — [The Tidewatch, part 2 chapter 1]
- A squat tower of black basalt with a lamp room of salt-crusted glass on top — [The Tidewatch, part 2 chapter 1]
- Two hundred and twelve steps spiral up inside it, with landings — [The Tidewatch, part 2 chapter 1]
- The lamp room holds the Mother Lamp: a salt lantern as tall as a man, its brine fed from a copper tank by a slow drip. Its light goes out over the harbor mouth in a wide pale fan, and where it touches the Murk the fog thins — [The Tidewatch, part 2 chapter 1]
- A lamp-room log is kept there; Corwin Ashby wrote in it — [The Tidewatch, Interlude: The Keeper's Log]
- On the storm night, the Mother Lamp was the last light still burning — [The Tidewatch, part 2 chapter 3]
- Iron rungs at its foot lead down to the old drowned quay at low tide — [The Drowned Bell, chapter 3]

## Reasonable inferences
- The Mother Lamp burns blue-white like the other salt lanterns — based on [The Tidewatch, part 1 chapter 1; part 2 chapter 1]

## Unknown / not established

## Continuity notes
